# Code of Conduct

We want participation in A Daraja CLI to be safe, respectful, and constructive.
Be considerate, assume good intent, welcome people with different backgrounds
and experience levels, and critique ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other
participation that violates these expectations and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not use a public
issue when doing so. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as
practical and handle reports fairly and promptly.
